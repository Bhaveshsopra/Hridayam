About AI Smart Tourism

AI Smart Tourism MP is an innovative digital platform designed to transform tourism in Madhya Pradesh. It combines Artificial Intelligence, real-time data and smart planning tools to create a safer and smarter travel experience.

Our system helps tourists with: ✔ AI Travel Itinerary Planning ✔ Tourist Safety Monitoring ✔ AI Budget Planner ✔ Weather & Crowd Prediction ✔ Multilingual AI Chatbot ✔ AI Voice Tour Guide

This project aims to promote safe, intelligent and technology-driven tourism across Madhya Pradesh.
